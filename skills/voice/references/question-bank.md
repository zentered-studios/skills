# Question bank

Two lists: what to read for in the samples, and what to ask that the
samples cannot show. Ask one question at a time. Skip any question the
samples or earlier answers already settle.

## What to read for

Record each trait with verbatim quotes. A trait with one quote is
`tentative`.

- **Openings.** First sentence of each sample: thesis, question, context,
  greeting, or straight to the point.
- **Closings.** Summary, call to action, sign-off, or a flat stop.
- **Sentence length.** Short and even, long and even, or high variance.
  Count a few to be sure.
- **Person.** I, we, you, or impersonal. Direct address to the reader.
- **Certainty.** Flat conclusions or hedges ("I think", "maybe", "might").
  How unknowns are stated.
- **Specificity.** Numbers, names, dates and file paths versus "a lot",
  "significant", "the system".
- **Structure.** Prose, bullets, headings, length of paragraphs. When
  bullets appear, are they real lists?
- **Register.** Formal, plain, chatty. Contractions. Profanity.
- **Humor.** Irony, dry asides, one-line punchlines, none.
- **Punctuation.** Em dashes, exclamation marks, semicolons, ellipses,
  Oxford comma, parentheses, emoji.
- **Spelling and case.** American or British, lowercase chat, title case
  headings.
- **Recurring words.** Words they use often and words that never appear.
- **Venue differences.** What changes between short-form and long-form.

## What to ask

### Readers and venues

- Who reads most of what you write? What do they need from it?
- Which venue matters most to get right? Which one do you rewrite most
  often?
- Do you write differently for your team, for customers and for strangers?
  How?

### What bothers you

- What makes you stop reading someone else's writing?
- When an AI drafts for you, what do you change first?
- Which words or phrases would you never use? Which ones do people
  overuse around you?

### Habits and conventions

- How do you say you are not sure about something?
- Do you open with context or with the point? Does that change by venue?
- How do you end a message? Sign-off, question, nothing?
- Any format rules you follow: commit message style, subject lines,
  headings, date formats, units?
- Emoji, exclamation marks, humor: where are they fine, where never?

### Aspiration versus habit

- Is there anything in your current writing you want to stop doing?
- Whose writing do you admire? What specifically do they do?

### Language

- Which spelling variant? Do you write in more than one language, and
  does the voice change between them?

## Contrast pairs

Write the same two or three sentences twice, changing one trait. Use the
person's own topic. Example traits to pair:

- Point first versus context first.
- Flat conclusion versus hedged conclusion.
- Prose versus bullets for the same content.
- Sign-off versus no sign-off.
- Short sentences versus one long joined sentence.
