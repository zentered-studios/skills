Each trait or rule the response states points to evidence in the three
messages: a verbatim quote, or a plain reference such as "all three". At
least three traits carry a verbatim quote. Traits are checkable by
pointing at a sentence (for example: lowercase, no greeting, exact
numbers instead of qualifiers). Marking a single-quote trait as tentative
is fine. The response fails if it states vague rules such as "be warm" or
"be concise" with no evidence, or quotes text that is not in the
messages.
